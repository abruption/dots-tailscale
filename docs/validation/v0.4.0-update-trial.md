## Limits

- The live path used a reviewed local development bundle. Successful download and application of a future manifest-bearing GitHub release remain to be checked after such a release is published. Invalid metadata, redirects, response limits and network failures are covered by offline tests.
- No deliberate VM destruction, power loss, SIGKILL, credential reset, or unknown-process termination was performed. Interrupted journals and write failures were exercised with controlled fixtures; they require manual review in production.
- `applied` proves manifest consistency and local readiness. Fresh external SSH/ping supply additional connectivity evidence for this trial, not all applications or future environments.
- The updater handles the fixed four-source bundle and restarts only shim. It refuses Tailscale binary-version changes and unknown/mixed legacy installations.
- After a crash, inspect leftover `.new` files and incomplete transactions before a manual retry. Preserve evidence and follow the documented archive/recovery procedure; do not clear a block blindly.
- The experimental watchdog cannot start itself after VM replacement. Its new-boot run is separate from the retired prior-boot ledger; no cross-VM recovery-budget continuity is claimed.

The healthy development candidate remained installed after the trial. No merge, release, or tag publication was performed as part of validation.
